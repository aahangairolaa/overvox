# OVERVOX v2.7.3 (27 Nov 2026)

Fixes from the v2.7.2 test run, plus the new map **Harbor Yard**.

## Fixes
- Overpower: the extra "VOX 25 · 23 RUST" score line is gone (only the blue/red bar shows).
- Overpower: stuck "Havoc" name tags no longer stay on screen; tags of bots that no longer exist are removed.
- Stuck crouch: held movement keys are cleared on every respawn and match start, and Shift/Ctrl are re-checked on mouse clicks.
- Crouch cooldown removed (you can slide again straight away).
- Shield hit marker is amber instead of blue.

## Enemies and sound
- Spider (skitter) sounds: quieter, lower, and fewer footsteps. The old sounds are still in the code (`sScreechOld`, `sRobotStepOld`) if we want them back.
- Hornets: 2.6× bigger, rusted metal, slower, fire less often, bolts do 5 damage instead of 9, and they buzz (heard from 35 m).

## Movement
- Zip line: Space lets go, you can grab it mid-air, and the F prompt shows when you look at the cable (not when looking down). Smooth snap onto the line.
- Jump pads: three short stacked rings instead of the tall blue column. They launch the way you are moving (30% pull toward the roof), sprint adds distance, and a slide in adds the most, capped so you land at the far end of the roof.

## Weapons and HUD
- Shotgun: 12 pellets × 12 damage, tighter spread (wide at long range), round crosshair that grows with the spread.
- Sniper: smaller crosshair when not scoped.
- Mouse side buttons and the scroll wheel can be bound in Settings → Keys (left and right click stay fixed).
- Death cam for online matches and robot waves: for 2 seconds your view turns to what killed you, with its name and HP left.
- Loadout: any slot can be set to NONE (two guns and no melee, or one gun and melee only). Number keys and scroll only cycle what you kept.
- Loading screen: GARY PRODUCTIONS in yellow, "Made by Aahan" in white. Version on the home screen: V2.7.3.

## New map: HARBOR YARD (120 × 120 m)
- West: two warehouses (mezzanine catwalks, roll-up doors) and a harbor office, joined by roof bridges.
- Middle: container yard with stacks of 1 to 3 (ladders on every stack) and a gantry crane with a 12 m walkway.
- East: the quay, two quay cranes, and a moored cargo ship you board by two long gangways or a jump pad. Ship deckhouse has two floors and a roof.
- 3 jump pads, 2 zip lines (crane cab to warehouse B roof, ship bridge to the quay), 12 spawns.
- Works in Bot Deathmatch, Overpower and online co-op (pick it with the MAP option).

## Tested
Headless runs: new-feature test (loadout, zip, pads, crosshairs, mouse button), Harbor Yard walk test (gangways, all 33 ladders, stairs, edges, pads, zips, spawns), bots on Harbor Yard for 60 s, smoke, Overpower solo and online, boss, online co-op on a map, map round trip, Overrun.
