# OVERVOX v2.8 (test builds 1 to 6, 27 Nov 2026) – includes v2.7.4

Not on GitHub yet. Live is v2.7.2. The newest build (v2.9 test 1) includes everything from v2.7.3, v2.7.4, v2.8 and v2.9.

## New map: BURIED CITY (172 m, desert old town on sloped sand)
- Streets of generated houses, Town Hall, Plaza Rosa arcade, Red Tower, apartments, stations, park, hatch, collapsed highway camp, overpass, leaning bus, far hills and pylons.
- Rope zip lines (6), bridges between roofs (straight, slanted, broken; mostly metal, a few short wooden planks), second roofs, broken walls and roof holes, half-buried houses, crates and generators to climb. No jump pads on this map.
- Works in Bot Deathmatch, Overpower and online co-op.

## Interiors (test 4)
- All 19 houses on Main Street and south of it are open: real doors and windows, stairs between floors, furnished rooms (living, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, study, storerooms, shops). Sand is dug away from the doorways.
- Every flat roof has a trapdoor with a steep wooden stair down to the top floor (no ladders).
- Plaza Rosa: pharmacy, café and grocer with storerooms; stairs to a first-floor corridor with flats and offices; roof trapdoor.
- Red Tower: stairs wind up inside past the windows to a trapdoor on the lookout.
- Southern Station: half-open shutter, ticket hall, booth, turnstiles, departures board, stairs down into the sand.
- Interiors are drawn only near you, so performance outside stays the same.

## Loading screen (tests 4 and 6)
- Shows before every match: map picture, map name and mode, a tip (keys follow your bindings), big LOADING bar bottom-left.
- Test 6: every map's picture is now a drone shot taken in the game, with a slow drone drift. Buried City tips only show on Buried City. Minimum 2 s (3 s from v2.9).

## Movement and climbing
- Ledge grab on every map, walkable and slidable roofs, zip drop keeps momentum, compass ticks.
- Easier roof climbing: reach 2.4 m, two-step crates and taller generators, roof-to-roof steps, more outside stairs and sand ramps.
- CLIMB prompt everywhere (including mid-air). SPACE climbs or vaults.
- Test 6: first-person climb. On a ledge grab or chest-high climb the gun drops out of view and two gloved hands grip the edge, press as you pull up, then let go. Ledge climb takes 0.67 s (was 0.54 s). Hopping over low cover keeps the old gun swing.

## Trailer mode (test 5, hidden)
- Settings → Gameplay → Secret code `gary` → Play trailer reel. 17 camera-only map cinematics plus 6 action shots, optional logo + COMING SOON card. Space starts, Esc quits. For recording only.

## Performance (test 3)
- 1 m ground mesh, rope parts merged, small details hidden beyond 70 m, 1.25x render cap on Buried City.

## v2.7.4 (folded in)
- Citadel pads without chevrons, nudged toward the clock tower; more force when running; bigger slide bonus.
- Command key refused with a warning; held-keys debug option; mouse/wheel binding hint; version on the loading screen.
