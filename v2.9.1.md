# VOXOVR v2.9.1 (28 Nov 2026)

**Type:** release · **Upload path:** `public/index.html`, `worker.js`, `social.js`, `wrangler.toml`, `v2.9.1.md` · Online version bumped: everyone refreshes the page (v2.9 players get "different game version").

## New name and look
- **OVERVOX is now VOXOVR.** It has a new logo, a new intro animation (Space skips), and a sign-in screen over a slideshow of all four maps.
- **New home screen:** red layout, with PLAY and the menu on the left. On the right, a slideshow of the exclusive outfits in their own poses: the Drifter with his revolver, Red Oni in a katana stance, and Oden with his arms crossed.
- **Top bar:** level, SCRAP (coins are now called scrap), FRIENDS, party faces, your name with an avatar, and full screen.

## Friends and party
- Add friends by username (up to 10) and see who's online, in a lobby or in a match.
- Party of up to 4. When the leader opens an online room, the whole party joins it.
- Your party stands next to you on the home screen in their own outfits.

## New weapon: STRAY (bow)
- Hold fire to draw (0.45 s) and let go to loose. The next arrow nocks itself.
  - A full-draw body hit does about 110 damage, and a headshot does far more.
- You can draw and shoot **while sprinting**, at full accuracy. The bow cants flat as you run.
- Arrows fly flat for 15 m and then drop gently. They stick in walls and the ground, and you get them back by walking over them.
- Aim assist: an arrow that passes close to a player's body counts as a hit. It's 12 cm close up and grows with distance. Headshots stay exact.
- Two holding styles: Settings → Controls → Bow.
- An optional **PIN SIGHT**: Loadout → Stray → ATTACHMENT.
- Skins: Hazard, Bone, Synthwave and Oni Lacquer. Your arrows fly in your skin's colours.
- A Stray headshot kill throws them into a backflip.

## Combat
- **Peek (lean):** Q / E by default.
  - Your view slides 50 cm and tilts.
  - By default it also opens your sights.
  - It pauses your sprint.
  - Other players see you lean, and your hitbox leans too.
- **Firing stops your sprint** (every weapon except the bow). Let go and you sprint again.
- **Sniper is bolt action:** after each shot you see and hear the bolt work, and you come off the scope while it cycles.
- **Knockback per weapon**, now online too:
  - Shotgun up close: 4.0.
  - Sniper: 2.5 body, 3.4 head.
  - Grenade: 3.78.
  - Stray: 2.2 body, 3.2 headshot.
- **Shotgun:** pellets that hit the same player count as one hit, which makes much less lag. Damage is unchanged.
- **Every mouse button can be bound**, including left and right click, so you can put Peek on a mouse button (Settings → Keys).
- **Loadouts:** 3 saved loadouts you can rename. In deathmatch, press **B** to switch loadout during spawn protection or while dead.
- **Spawn protection in deathmatch:** 7 s. It ends after 10 m of movement or your first shot.
- **Kill cam online:** when another player kills you, you see a replay.

## Settings

## Fixes
- Missing elbows on characters: the forearm now always meets the elbow.
- A freeze on the first shot of a match: effects are now warmed up behind the loading screen.
- A crash on old or unknown saved outfits or loadouts. The game now falls back to defaults instead of crashing.
- Lists in Customize, Loadout, the store and mode select now scroll.
- A stretched arm after a ledge climb or vault.
- Lag when standing close to other players. Players are now solid.
- Not respawning in online deathmatch. Spawns now vary.
- Toggle sprint no longer stops by itself.
- Buried City runs smoother.
- If you get stuck inside geometry, the game moves you to a free spot.
- No name tags in free-for-all deathmatch.
- A new match loading screen with the map name, coordinates and region.
