# OVERVOX v2.9 (28 Nov 2026)

**Type:** release · **Upload path:** `public/index.html`, `worker.js`, `accounts.js`, `wrangler.toml`, `patch-notes/` · Includes everything from v2.7.3, v2.7.4 and v2.8.

## Release changes (after test 1)
- Levelling: levels 1–5 come quickly as before, then each level takes much more XP (2,000 + 300 × (level − 4)²). Roughly: level 5 after ~3 matches, level 10 after ~25, level 15 after ~100.
- Test buttons (+5,000 coins, +2,000 XP) removed from Career.
- Online version bumped: players still on v2.7.x get "different game version" instead of a broken match. Everyone refreshes the page.
- Version shows as V2.9.

## Opening and sign-in
- New opening: quick drone glimpses of Outpost, Citadel and Harbor Yard (1 s each), stopping on Buried City, with the OVERVOX logo. LOADING sits bottom-left.
- Then a sign-in screen: **Sign in**, **Create account**, or **Play as guest**. Signed-in players go straight to the menu.
- Accounts: username + password (stored only as a secure hash). Level, coins, skins, outfits and loadout save to your account and follow you to any device. Settings stay per device.
- Fresh start: everyone's progress starts again from level 1.

## Levels 1–50
- A reward at every level from 2 to 15: five new MK-7 Ranger skins (Hazard, Rust Belt, Game Show, Salvage, Circuit) and five new outfits (Contestant, Holo Runner, Scavenger, Surface Crew, Nomad), then Phantom (12) and Ghillie (14).
- Older skins unlock from 16 to 35, gold skins from 36 to 45 (AK gold at 45).
- Level 50: gold knife, the black-and-gold LEGEND outfit and the OVERVOX LEGEND title.
- Coins every level, rising from 150 to 600.
- 25 titles to unlock; pick yours in Career. It shows under your name.
- Basic Soldier, Blue SWAT and Black Ops are free from the start.
- Level-up screen when you get back to the menu: what you unlocked and what's next.
- The home screen card now shows your next reward and an XP bar.
- Career shows the whole 1–50 reward track.

## Store
- New Featured tab with the new skins and outfits.
- Locked items show the level they unlock at. Wear outfits straight from the store.
- Premium items paid by UPI QR (switched on once items, prices and the QR are added). Orders are checked and approved by hand.

## Play screen
- Pick a mode from big picture cards, then (Bot Deathmatch, Overpower) pick the map from drone-shot thumbnails with the bot settings, then DEPLOY.
- Future modes are shown in a "Coming later" row.
- Map loading screen stays up at least 3 s.
